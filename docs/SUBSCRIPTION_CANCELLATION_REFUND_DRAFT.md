## Subscription policy

IntelliDocs Pro is a monthly subscription with a base price of RM50. Your checkout will show the total payable, including any applicable taxes, before you confirm payment. [Owner must decide and configure whether RM50 includes applicable taxes.]

Your card subscription renews automatically each month on the billing date shown in your account unless cancelled before the renewal. Each successful paid renewal provides a new monthly AI allowance. The allowance is shared across document processing and questions; consumption depends on the processing performed, including the models and amount of content involved. It is not an unlimited-use subscription or RM50 of cash credit.

Unused allowance does not roll over and cannot be withdrawn as cash. Requests may be blocked when the remaining allowance is insufficient. Your account displays your current allowance usage and billing period. Reaching the allowance limit does not itself cancel your subscription or stop the next renewal.

Any material change to pricing or allowance terms will be communicated before it applies to a future renewal, allowing you to cancel before that renewal. [Owner must approve the notice arrangements and implement them before relying on this clause.]

## Cancellation policy

You can cancel automatic renewal through Manage subscription in IntelliDocs. If you cannot access it, contact hello.intellidocs@gmail.com. Retain the cancellation confirmation and check the displayed service end date.

Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of your current paid billing period. You retain access for the remainder of that period, subject to your remaining allowance and the service terms. No new monthly subscription charge should be taken after cancellation becomes effective.

Cancellation does not automatically delete your account, uploaded documents or conversations. Data retention and deletion are governed by our separate privacy policy. Cancelling renewal and requesting a refund are separate actions.

If you believe a charge was taken after an effective cancellation, contact us with the invoice/payment reference so we can investigate. Do not send full card details.

## Refund policy — simplified draft

Your applicable statutory rights are not limited by this policy.

We will refund confirmed duplicate or incorrect charges. If a paid service was not delivered or a material service failure occurred, contact us so we can investigate and determine the appropriate remedy, which may include a refund.

Send refund requests to hello.intellidocs@gmail.com with your account email, invoice/payment reference and a short explanation. Do not send card numbers, security codes or document content unless specifically needed through an appropriate secure channel.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where supported. The time until funds appear depends on the payment provider and bank.

Subscription cancellation and any effect of a refund on paid access or remaining allowance will be confirmed separately.
